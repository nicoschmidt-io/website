Since the 2023 reform of the Energy Efficiency Directive, operators of large data centres have to report to the EU how much energy and water their facilities use. The Commission's first draft said the data would be published in aggregated form. Then Microsoft and the industry group DigitalEurope proposed almost identical wording: information on individual sites should count as commercially sensitive. The Commission adopted it nearly word for word.

Since then, only national totals have been public. Residents, researchers and journalists can't find out what a single data centre consumes, not even through freedom of information requests. Ten legal scholars told us this may breach the Aarhus Convention and the EU Charter of Fundamental Rights. Meanwhile, the EU wants to roughly triple its data centre capacity within five years.
