Google, Microsoft and Amazon are building huge data centres all over Europe. The actual building is often done by migrant workers, many from Romania, hired through long chains of subcontractors. Together with partner outlets, we spent five months looking at conditions on sites in Sweden, Norway, Belgium and the Netherlands.

Workers told us about an electrocution that left a man with severe burns at a Google site in Belgium, rat-infested housing, a suspected tuberculosis outbreak and 60-hour weeks without overtime pay. Unions say they can barely get onto the sites; the companies point to their supplier codes. One small win: 78 Romanian workers at a Microsoft site in Sweden are now getting €1.3 million in back pay and damages.
