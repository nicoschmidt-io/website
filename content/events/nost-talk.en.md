 In Berlin I’ll join Sabrina Faramarzi (Are We Europe) and Alexandra Zeitlin (n-ost) to discuss how cross-border journalism turns local stories—climate protests, migration, politics—into a shared European conversation. We’ll examine how reporters balance local relevance with an EU angle, where progress shows and which gaps remain.
