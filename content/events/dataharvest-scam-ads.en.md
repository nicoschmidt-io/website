A famous face recommends a can't-lose investment, except the video is fake. AI-generated scam ads like this are flooding social media across Europe, even though the Digital Services Act is supposed to stop them. In this session we show how to track such campaigns with Meta's Ad Library, open tools and leaked material, and how to estimate their scale. And how to get from the data to the people who lost their money.
