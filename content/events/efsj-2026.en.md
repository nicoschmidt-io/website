From AI to the climate crisis, the stories that define our time don't stop at national borders, and neither should journalism. In this online workshop for the European Federation for Science Journalism, I draw on my work at Investigate Europe and as an AI Accountability Fellow at the Pulitzer Center: why cross-border collaboration matters more than ever, why it is easier than it looks, and what it can uncover that no single newsroom could on its own.
