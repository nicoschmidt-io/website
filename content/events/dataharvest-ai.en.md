 Europe’s new AI Act promises oversight, yet loopholes remain in surveillance, policing and algorithmic workplace control. In this workshop I reveal how I track AI systems hiding in plain sight: biometric cameras, productivity scoring, risk profiling. You’ll learn how the Act creates openings for investigation, where it leaves blind spots, and how to follow lobbying, file FOI requests and expose the concrete impact of automated decisions that authorities would rather keep invisible.
