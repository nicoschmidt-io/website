The AI race is putting hundreds of new data centres on land and in communities around the world, often with big promises and very little information for the people living next door. Data Center Bombs, a platform by Data for Good, maps these projects together with affected communities. In this lab session at Mozilla Festival we look at how the map works, collect the places, actors and stories that are still missing, and write up a few of them on the spot.
