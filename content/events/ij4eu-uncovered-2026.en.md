Five minutes for a whole investigation. In the "Cross-border Snapshots" session at the IJ4EU conference at the Goethe-Institut Budapest, I explain how Microsoft and the tech industry's lobby wrote secrecy into EU rules, which is why we still can't see how much energy and water individual data centres use. The investigation was funded by IJ4EU. After the short talks, the audience gets to ask questions.
