*Last update: 12 Jul 2025*

### 1 · General  
This site collects as little personal data as possible. No cookies, no analytics, no third-party trackers.

### 2 · Server logs  
When you visit the site, Cloudflare (hosting provider) automatically stores: IP address, date/time, requested file, referrer and user agent. Logs are deleted after 14 days and are not merged with other data.

### 3 · Contact via E-mail  
If you contact me via Proton Mail, your message is stored solely to process your request and will not be shared with third parties.

### 4 · Your rights (GDPR)  
You have the right to access, rectify or delete your personal data at any time. Supervisory authority: Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

### 5 · Changes  
This policy will be updated whenever legal or technical changes make it necessary.
